Roadmap To The World of Nanotech

Nanotechnology combines physics, chemistry, biology, and engineering. This roadmap helps you learn step-by-step.

  1. Nano Basics — learn the foundation and key ideas.
  2. Nanomaterials — explore special materials and their properties.
  3. Applications — learn how nanotech is used in the real world.
  4. Projects & Research — apply what you have learned.

Available Nanotechnology Courses

📘 Course 1: Nanotechnology Fundamentals

This course introduces the nanoscale world and explains nanotechnology in a simple and clear way.

What you will cover:
  • What is nanotechnology?
  • Nanoscale size comparisons
  • Surface area effect
  • Nano properties and behavior
  • Everyday nano products
Beginner • 2–3 hours

🧪 Course 2: Nanomaterials

Explore important nanomaterials and understand their properties, strengths, and real-world uses.

What you will cover:
  • Graphene and why it is special
  • Carbon nanotubes
  • Gold and silver nanoparticles
  • Quantum dots
  • Nanocoatings
Intermediate • 3 hours

🩺 Course 3: Nanotechnology in Medicine

Learn how nanoparticles can deliver medicine, detect diseases earlier, and improve treatments.

What you will cover:
  • Nanoparticles for drug delivery
  • Cancer treatment and nano-therapy
  • Nano biosensors
  • Antibacterial coatings
  • Future nano-medicine ideas
Intermediate • 3–4 hours

💻 Course 4: Nano Electronics

Learn how nano-sized transistors and nanomaterials are shaping future devices.

What you will cover:
  • Nano transistors and chips
  • Why smaller chips are faster
  • Quantum dots in displays
  • Flexible electronics
  • Future computing materials
Intermediate • 3–4 hours

⚡ Course 5: Energy & Environment

Explore nano-based solutions for batteries, solar panels, clean water, and environmental protection.

What you will cover:
  • Nanotech batteries and supercapacitors
  • Nano solar cell technology
  • Water purification and nano filters
  • Nano catalysts
  • Environmental impacts
Beginner • 2 hours

🧩 Course 6: Projects, Research & Skills

Apply what you’ve learned by reading research papers, understanding lab tools, and planning projects.

What you will cover:
  • Research skills and scientific writing
  • SEM/TEM microscopy basics
  • Understanding scientific papers
  • Project ideas and planning
  • Building a learning portfolio
Advanced • 3–4 hours
